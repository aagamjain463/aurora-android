## App name (30)

Aurora: Focus & App Blocker

## Short description (80)

Block distracting apps, open apps on purpose, and see real time back. On-device.

## Full description (4000)

Your phone. Your rules.

Aurora helps you choose when to open the apps that pull you away, and protects the time you want back. It
works on your phone: no account, no ads, no analytics. Your usage never leaves it.

SHIELD: HOLD BACK THE APPS THAT PULL YOU AWAY
Choose apps and websites, then pick how firmly Aurora holds them back:
• Flexible: blocked until your timer ends, or you turn it off.
• Friction: complete a challenge before getting access.
• Cooldown: wait before you can open the app.
• Always Block: no in-app early exit. Protection ends when your timer does.

INTENT: OPEN APPS ON PURPOSE
For the apps you open out of habit, Aurora pauses for a moment, asks why and for how long, and follows
through when your time is up with a reminder or a cooldown.

SESSIONS FOR YOUR DAY
Combine a Shield, an Intent and an optional Focus session. Start one now, later, on a weekly schedule, or
keep it on. Saved sessions start from Home in one tap.

TRACK: REAL PROGRESS
Goals for your sessions and app limits, streaks, verified time protected and screen time, measured on your
phone from Android's own records. Never invented numbers.

AURORA OUTSIDE THE APP
A quiet ongoing notification with the time left, a home-screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your usage stays on your phone and is never backed up to the cloud. Aurora only goes online to confirm an Aurora Plus purchase.

AURORA FREE AND AURORA PLUS
Aurora Free protects just as strongly as Plus, with a few limits: 5 active Shield apps and sites, 3 active
Intent apps, 1 active goal and 2 hours a day shared by Cooldown and Always Block. Focus, Flexible and Friction
Shields, schedules, Daily Limits and presets are unlimited. Aurora Plus removes the limits and adds 30-day insights. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Aurora uses Android's accessibility service to notice which app is in front, so it can pause the apps you
choose. It reads only the name of that app. The optional Web & content Shield reads the address bar in
supported browsers, to pause websites you protect, and notices Shorts or Reels if you ask it to. Aurora
explains this before you turn anything on, and nothing it sees leaves your phone.
