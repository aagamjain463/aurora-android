Aurora privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Aurora is a digital wellbeing app for Android. It helps you choose when to open the apps that pull you away. Aurora is built to work entirely on your phone: it has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no permission to use the internet.

What Aurora keeps, and where

Aurora stores your settings and your progress in its private storage on your phone: the apps and websites you protect, your Shields, Intents, sessions, schedules and Goals, your Focus sessions, daily counts and the reasons you write. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. It is excluded from Google's cloud backup. You can see what is stored, clear your history or delete everything in Settings › Privacy & data.

Accessibility services

Aurora Shield uses Android's accessibility service only to notice which app comes to the front, so it can pause the apps you chose. It reads the name of that app and nothing else. It does not read the screen, your messages or what you type.

Aurora Web & content Shield is optional and off unless you turn it on. In supported browsers it reads the address in the address bar, to pause websites you protect. In YouTube and Instagram it checks the names of on-screen elements, only to notice Shorts or Reels if you asked for that. It does not read messages, passwords or what you type, and it does not store or send what it sees.

Aurora explains each service and asks before opening Android's settings. Only you can turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings › Accessibility.

Usage access

If you allow it, Aurora reads Android's usage records on your phone to show your screen time, your time away from protected apps and your daily limits. These records stay on your phone.

Uninstall protection

If you turn it on, Aurora becomes a device administrator with no policies, only so it cannot be uninstalled while a strict commitment you started is running. You can turn it off whenever no commitment is running.

Notifications

With your permission, Aurora shows a quiet ongoing notification while protection or Focus runs. It is created on your phone and contains only the name of what is running and its time left.

Purchases

Aurora Plus is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy. Aurora only asks Google Play, on your phone, whether you have an active subscription. We never see your payment details.

Children

Aurora is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Because Aurora collects nothing, a change can never apply to data collected earlier.
