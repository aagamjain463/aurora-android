Aurora privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Aurora is a digital wellbeing app for Android. It helps you choose when to open the apps that pull you away and protects the time you want back. Aurora enforces all protection strictly on your phone, with zero analytics, zero crash-reporting trackers, and no advertising. Verified accounts and cloud synchronization exist solely to bind your purchases, share your Free quota across your devices, and prevent lock circumvention.

1. What Aurora keeps on your phone

Aurora stores your personal settings and habit records in private storage on your device: the specific apps and websites you protect, your Shields, Intents, reflection notes, motivations, session history, and Focus timers. None of this personal usage data is ever sent to our servers or to third parties. It is excluded from Google's cloud backup. You can inspect your stored data, clear your history, or reset everything at any time in Settings › Privacy & data.

2. Accounts and cloud synchronization

Aurora requires a verified account to synchronize your plan and protect your commitments:

  • Authentication: You sign in using Google Sign-In or a passwordless Email Link handled via Firebase Authentication. We store your account identifier (UID), verified email address, and display name.
  • Devices & Installations: A random installation identifier is generated when Aurora is installed, to bind authorizations and manage multi-device seat limits (up to 5 concurrent active devices for Aurora Plus). It is not a hardware identifier and is replaced if you clear Aurora's data or reinstall it. Aurora also creates a signing key on your phone, which never leaves it; our server keeps only its public half, to confirm requests really come from that installation. Aurora never reads your IMEI, serial number, advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • One allowance per phone: Aurora Free's daily allowance is shared by every account used on the same installation, so it can't be renewed by switching accounts. For this our server records which installation each committed interval was on. If an account is deleted, the last three days of those intervals stay with the installation, with nothing that identifies the account, and are then erased.
  • App and device integrity: Where available, Aurora occasionally asks Google Play Integrity whether it is the genuine app on a genuine device. We keep only the outcome (for example "genuine" or "failed") with the installation, never the token. An unavailable check is never held against you.
  • Free Quota Synchronization: On Aurora Free, strict commitments (Strict and Cooldown) share a 120-minute daily allowance across your devices. To verify this allowance, Aurora sends the commitment duration, start timestamp, and your local time zone to our Cloudflare-hosted backend. No app package names, website domains, or personal reflections are ever included in quota requests.
  • Aurora Plus Purchases: When you subscribe to Aurora Plus via Google Play, your purchase token is verified with the Google Play Developer API and bound to your verified Aurora account.

3. Accessibility services

Aurora Shield uses Android's accessibility service solely to observe which application moves to the foreground, allowing it to pause the apps you chose to limit. It inspects only the package identifier of the frontmost app. It does not read your screen, inspect messages, or capture keystrokes.

Aurora Web & content Shield is optional and disabled by default. In supported web browsers, it reads the address in the URL bar to pause protected domains. In YouTube and Instagram, it inspects UI element IDs strictly to detect Shorts or Reels if you opted into that protection. It never reads personal messages, credentials, or typed input, and it never transmits what it inspects.

4. Usage access

If you grant Usage Access, Aurora reads Android's on-device system records (`UsageStatsManager`) to display your screen time, daily usage limits, and time spent away from distracting apps. These records remain entirely on your device.

5. Uninstall protection

If enabled, Aurora requests device administrator privileges with no security policies (``), solely to prevent uninstallation while an active strict commitment timer is counting down. It can be deactivated whenever no strict session is running.

6. Data retention and account deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your account and associated cloud data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings › Account › Delete account. After confirming via reauthentication, your account enters a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel if desired. Local data is immediately wiped from your phone.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit a deletion request via our account deletion page.
  • Data Purge: Once the grace period expires, all account records, device bindings, purchase links, and quota ledger entries are permanently erased from our databases, except the anonymous installation intervals described above, which are erased within three days.
  • Anti-abuse records: Records of suspected misuse (for example, an installation refused for using another installation's identifier) name only the installation and account, are erased after 30 days or with your account, and never lead to a ban. If you think Aurora wrongly limited you, write to support and we will review and correct it.
  • Installation records (the random identifier, its public key and the integrity outcome) are erased after 180 days without use.

7. Children

Aurora is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
