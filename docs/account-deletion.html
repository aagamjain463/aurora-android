Google Play Data Safety Deletion Portal for Aurora

You can request the permanent deletion of your Aurora account and all associated cloud data. If you have the Aurora app installed, the quickest method is directly within the app under Settings › Account › Delete account.

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your Android device, you may submit a manual deletion request below.

Submit a deletion request

What happens next: We will verify ownership by sending a confirmation link to the email address above. Once verified, your account and all associated device records, quota ledgers, and purchase links will be permanently erased following our standard 30-day grace period. Active Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled through Google Play Subscriptions.

Data Types Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Firebase authentication records, account UID, verified email address, registered device installation IDs, and strict quota ledgers.
  • On-Device Local Data: App blocking lists, website lists, personal reflections, and motivations remain solely on your physical device and are never possessed by Aurora's servers; clearing local app storage removes them completely.
  • Financial Records: Transaction receipts are processed and retained by Google Play in accordance with applicable tax and commercial law.
