## Approved product decision

Free includes 120 Always Block minutes per day. A scheduled Always Block window commits the allowance still available; the rest of that window behaves as Flexible. The wizard discloses this fallback. The founder approved keeping this behaviour on 29 October 2026. Plus commits the full window. Existing commitments survive downgrade.
