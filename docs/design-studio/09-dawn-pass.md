# 09: The Dawn pass

30 October 2026. The founder asked for a rating of the whole brand and for significant changes. This redesign
changes the approved First Light identity in the ways listed here, at the founder's request.

## Rating

| Area | Before | After | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept and ownable idea | 8 | 9.5 | The ring in the art is now the app's clock, and the symbol forms in the sky |
| Logo and wordmark | 6.5 | 8.5 | Newsreader wordmark; the symbol itself is unchanged |
| Colour | 7 | 9 | Solid Starlight action instead of a mint gradient; emerald only means "on" |
| Typography | 7.5 | 9.5 | Fine Newsreader numerals, larger display sizes, sentence-case eyebrows |
| Art | 7 | 9 | Richer sky, glowing ring, higher-quality export (WebP q92) |
| Layout | 6.5 | 9 | Shield and Focus action under the thumb, no dead space, no ring within a ring, Home cut down |
| Motion | 6.5 | 9 | Calm controls, no blur or bounce, no replay on tabs, one signature move |
| Overall | 7 | 9 | |

The after column is the design team's judgement, not user research. A 10 needs what design alone cannot give:
real people using it, reliable enforcement on many phones, and art direction by a human illustrator.

## Not changed

Founder-approved copy, the symbol, the palette values, and all enforcement behaviour.
