# 02 — Brand identity

## Positioning

Aurora is a premium Android app for intentional technology use: it protects time from the apps that
pull people away, interrupts unconscious opening, and holds space for focused work.

**Brand promise:** *Your phone. Your rules.* (Founder-approved, the Today headline.)

**Personality:** calm, confident, honest, warm. Never punitive, never preachy, never fake.

## Concept: First Light

Darkness is automatic behaviour; light is awareness and choice. The identity is the moment the first
light of day appears over a horizon: a ring of light resting on the horizon, and one point of light
rising above it. The point is the person.

## Symbol

`brand/logo/aurora-symbol.svg` (master, generated by `brand/tools/logo.py`).

- 24-unit grid. Ring: centre (12, 19.2), radius 7.5, stroke 3, flat ends sitting on the horizon,
  emerald gradient `#2FB883 → #8BE8BF`. Point: centre (12, 6.2), radius 2, sun gold `#F3C77D`.
- Why this symbol, against the brief's criteria: it is built from the approved art's own ring, so it is
  recognisably Aurora; it has two shapes, so it stays legible at 16dp and in one colour; it avoids the
  generic sunrise (no disc, no rays, no waves). It already appeared in the approved references, so it
  was kept and refined rather than replaced. Trademark distinctiveness has not been searched; a
  clearance search is an external step before registration.
- Variants: `aurora-symbol-mono-white.svg`, `aurora-symbol-mono-black.svg`.
- Clear space: one point-diameter (2 units) on every side. Minimum size: 16dp.
- In code: `AuroraMark(size)` in `ui/components/Brand.kt`, same geometry.

## Wordmark and lockups

"Aurora", title case, Manrope Medium (500), tracking −1%. Always beside or below the symbol, never
restyled, never lowercase in copy (`strings.xml` now says "Aurora" and "Aurora Plus" everywhere).

- `aurora-wordmark-light.svg` / `aurora-wordmark-dark.svg` (outlined from the bundled Manrope, so they
  render without the font)
- `aurora-lockup-on-night.svg` / `aurora-lockup-on-paper.svg`
- In code: `Wordmark()`, and `AuroraHeader()` (symbol + wordmark + Settings) on tab screens.

## App icon

Adaptive icon: `res/drawable/ic_launcher_background.xml` (night sky gradient with light pooling low),
`ic_launcher_foreground.xml` (the symbol on its 24-unit grid at 2.4x, centred in the safe zone),
`ic_launcher_monochrome.xml` (themed icons).
Master: `brand/logo/aurora-app-icon-512.svg`. Verified at launcher scale on the emulator
(`evidence/emulator/launcher-drawer.png`): it stays legible and distinct next to stock icons.

## Typography

Newsreader 400 is the editorial display face; Manrope is the interface, metric and wordmark face.
Both are bundled under SIL OFL. Display weights are deliberately light: confidence through
size, not weight.

| Style | Size/line | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| DisplayXL | 56/60 | 400 | Newsreader: short titles over art, such as "Focus" and "Track" |
| Display | 40/44 | 400 | Newsreader: editorial hero headlines (two-tone) |
| Metric | 56/60 | 500, tnum | The one number a card is about |
| Hero | 60/64 | 500, tnum | Timers |
| Headline / Title | 26/32, 19/25 | 620 | Card and section titles |
| Body / Secondary | 16/24, 15/21 | 450 | Copy |
| Label | 16/20 | 620 | Buttons |
| Caption / Eyebrow | 13 | 550 / 650 tracked | Supporting labels |

## Icon language

Own line icons (`ui/components/Icons.kt`): 24dp grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps, no fills. The Today tab
icon is the symbol itself. Icons sit in `LitBadge` rings when they stand for a state.

## Illustration language

Only First Light. No people, mascots, stock illustration or confetti. Data is drawn as light: bars that
warm toward gold for today, week dots that light up, a ring that closes.

## Motion language

See `04-design-system.md` → Motion. Short, eased, confirming; one slow breath at most; everything snaps
with reduced motion.

## Voice

Plain, warm, specific, second person. Founder-approved strings are fixed (see `CopyTest`). Never
"Lockdown", never guilt.
